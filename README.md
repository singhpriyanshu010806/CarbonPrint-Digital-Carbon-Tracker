# 🌱 CarbonPrint – Digital Carbon Tracker

CarbonPrint is a digital carbon footprint tracking platform designed to help users monitor, analyze, and reduce their carbon emissions. The project aims to promote environmental awareness and sustainable decision-making through data-driven insights and interactive features.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

With increasing climate change concerns, individuals and organizations often lack accessible tools to understand their carbon footprint. CarbonPrint addresses this gap by providing a simple yet effective way to calculate emissions and encourage eco-friendly practices.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Track and estimate carbon emissions based on user activities
* Provide insights and visual feedback on carbon impact
* Promote sustainable and environmentally responsible behavior
* Create awareness about carbon footprint reduction

---

## 🚀 Features

* 🌍 Carbon footprint calculation
* 📊 Emission analysis and insights
* 🧠 Awareness-driven recommendations
* 🖥️ User-friendly interface
